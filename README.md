### 🚀 About Me

I work at the intersection of **AI for Science** and **Low-Level Systems Programming**, with a strong emphasis on empirical AI evaluation and frontier model safety.

My current research interests are:

- 🧬 **AI for Biology (AI4Bio)** — molecular deep learning, graph neural networks for drug discovery, Bayesian optimization over chemical spaces, protein structure and function prediction.
- ⚡ **AI for Electronics (AI4Electronics)** — generative models and ML-assisted design for electronic systems, signal processing, and hardware-aware neural architectures.
- 🛡️ **Reinforcement Learning & Agent Safety** — building empirical RL evaluation harnesses for frontier models (GPT-5.5), agentic benchmarking, and safety-critical reward design.

I believe robust AI systems require full-stack fluency — from memory layout, POSIX syscalls, and OS kernels all the way up to frontier model training and evaluation pipelines.
